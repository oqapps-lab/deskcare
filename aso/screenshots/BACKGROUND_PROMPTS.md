## Tips for the generator session

- Use MidJourney v7 or SDXL with high coherence. Run each prompt 4× (`--repeat 4` or batch) — pick the best of 4 per prompt.
- For each, the goal is **a usable background** behind 6 phone mockups. Avoid prompts that put strong subjects in the center where phones will sit.
- After generation, crop / upscale to exact 7740×2796.
- Mix-and-match: not every screenshot needs the same background tone. Pick the
  background per frame's emotional beat (pain phase → warmer/burnt; relief
  phase → lighter peach/cream; outcome → cream + sage accent).
